Swing analysis on your iPhone

Your swing, honestly rated.

Film one swing. TrueTen measures it on your phone and gives you a strict rating out of 10, always with its margin of error, then a weekly plan built around the one change that matters most.

Strict, and honest about it

6.4 / 10 · ± 1.0

Only tour-level swings score 10. Every number comes with its margin, the way a measurement should, and anything the phone can't judge precisely enough is left out of your rating instead of guessed.

What you get

One camera is enough

Face-on from about 3 metres, hands-free: tap record, walk to the ball, swing. Add a down-the-line clip if you like, for swing path and hand depth.

A plan that fits your week

Your days, your minutes, where you practise and what you have. One swing change at a time, with drills and a re-film checkpoint judged against its margin.

Practice mode

Every swing measured as you hit balls, a slow-motion replay and a spoken cue, so you can keep your eyes on the ball, not the screen.

Progress you can trust

Ratings over time with their margins, checkpoints passed, practice streaks, a monthly report and side-by-side comparisons.

Private by design

  • Your swing is analysed on your iPhone. Videos stay on the phone unless you back them up to your own iCloud.
  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don't run servers that hold your data.
  • Export everything, or delete everything, from Settings › Your data.

Coaching information, not medical advice. Stop any drill that causes pain.
