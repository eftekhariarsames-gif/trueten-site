TrueTen beta

Device check

Thanks for helping. This checks that TrueTen works on real iPhones before launch: about 15 minutes at home, plus 30 minutes of Practice mode the next time you're at a range, net or simulator. Please send the results block at the bottom back to Arsames by text or email.

Goal: five different iPhone models, including an iPhone 11 or an iPhone SE (2nd or 3rd generation) if anyone has one.

Pass bar: no crashes · first result in under 2 minutes · Practice mode usable for 30 minutes.

Before you start (1 minute)

  1. Install TrueTen from the TestFlight link. If it's already installed, delete it first so you see the first-run setup.
  2. Charge to at least 80%. Turn Low Power Mode off (Settings › Battery).
  3. Have a stopwatch ready on another device, or use a friend's phone.
  4. Write down your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings › General › About).

If the app crashes at any point: reopen it. TestFlight usually asks to send a crash report: tap Share. Then write down what you were doing in the results block.

Part 1: first result (5 minutes)

  1. Start the stopwatch the moment you tap the TrueTen icon for the first time.
  2. Go through setup as you normally would. When it offers the sample swing, take it.
  3. Note the stopwatch time when the sample's rating appears: that's T-sample. Keep the stopwatch running.
  4. Tap Film my first swing. Allow the camera. Put the phone on a stand or lean it on something, face-on, about 3 m (10 ft) away, whole body in view. A real club isn't needed indoors: an alignment stick or a slow practice swing with no ball works.
  5. Leave the record screen's settings as they are (hands-free, 3 swings). Tap record, make your 3 swings, holding each finish; it stops by itself. Note the stopwatch time when your rating appears: that's T-first.
  6. Also note roughly how long the "Analysing" screen showed: that's T-analysis.

Part 2: quick checks (9 minutes)

Tick each one that works, and describe anything that doesn't.

  1. Results: scroll the whole result. Open the top fix and its drill. Nothing cut off or overlapping?
  2. Share card: tap the share icon (top right). Does the card look right?
  3. Practice tab: your plan appears with this week's focus and the first session.
  4. Paywall: tap any lock. Prices show (in your currency). Close it with the ✕. Don't subscribe: this is a test build, and purchases here use Apple's test system, so nothing is charged anyway.
  5. From library: pick any slow-motion video of a swing, if you have one. Does it analyse or explain why not?
  6. Settings (gear icon): everything opens.
  7. Background: go to the Home Screen for a minute, come back. Still where you left it?

Part 3: Practice mode, 30 minutes (next practice session)

At a range, net or simulator, with the phone on a stand face-on:

  1. Write down the battery % and the time. Screen brightness: leave it as you normally have it.
  2. Start Practice mode (Swings tab › Practice mode). Hit balls as normal for 30 minutes.
  3. For 5 swings spread through the session, time from holding your finish to hearing the spoken cue: that's the lag for each swing.
  4. Note anything that went wrong: missed swings, a swing counted twice, the replay freezing, the phone getting hot, a temperature warning, the app closing.
  5. At 30 minutes, end the session and write down the battery % again.

Usable means: it kept working for the full 30 minutes, caught most swings, the lag stayed under about 5 seconds, and the phone didn't overheat or shut the camera off.
